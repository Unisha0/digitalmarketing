Featured story

Mingma David Sherpa: a name written above the clouds

A 90 second film that turns a lifetime above 8,000 metres into one story: where he comes from, what he has done and why it matters to the next generation.

The man on the mountain

Mingma David Sherpa grew up in the Makalu region of eastern Nepal and became one of the finest high altitude climbers of his generation. He has stood on the summit of all fourteen of the world’s 8,000 metre peaks, becoming the youngest person ever to complete them, and was part of the all Nepali team that made the first winter ascent of K2 in January 2021.

Today he shares that journey with students and young Nepalis: the discipline, the risk and the belief it takes to climb where few people ever stand.

How we told it

  • Open on the legend. Cinematic title cards over his own summit footage set the scale before a word is spoken.
  • Show the climb. Ladders, ridges and frozen faces make 8,000 metres feel real, not just a number.
  • Let him speak. A calm studio interview on a clean set puts the focus on the person behind the records.
  • End with purpose. The film closes in a packed auditorium, where his story inspires the next generation.
