How we make it

From first workshop to finished kit

Four stages, usually four to eight weeks depending on scope.

02

Two or three logo and look and feel routes, each presented with the thinking behind it.

03

Build the system

04

Kit and handover

Everything packed into your brand kit and guidelines, with ready files for print, web and social.
