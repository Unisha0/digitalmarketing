What services does Trend Crafters offer?

Digital marketing (social media, Meta ads, TikTok, SEO), branding & campaigns, photo and video production, and IT solutions (web development, web apps, and maintenance), all under one roof.

Do you only work with businesses in Nepal?

No. While we're based in Kathmandu and dominate the local market, we execute campaigns and production for international clients as well.

What makes your visual work different?

We create premium, realistic visuals with cinematic lighting, lifelike textures, and refined details, delivering an authentic, sophisticated look beyond flat or overly CGI-driven designs.

Can you manage our social media from strategy to posting?

Yes, strategy, content production, posting, community management, and reporting, fully managed by our team.

Do you build the website too, or just market it?

Both. Our IT Solutions team builds custom, mobile-first websites and web apps, then our marketing team drives qualified traffic to them, so your funnel actually converts.

How do I get started?

Reach out through our contact form and tell us your business goal, we'll map out a plan tailored to your brand.
